Privacy Policy for QuranScroll

Last Updated: August 23, 2026

QuranScroll is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app accesses, what remains on your device, what limited information is transmitted when you use online or purchase features, and how that information is handled.

1. Overview

QuranScroll does not require you to create an account. The app does not use third-party advertising SDKs, does not sell personal information, and does not upload your Quran reading activity, daily reading goals, bookmarks, streaks, reading statistics, Focus App Blocker selections, or personal app preferences to QuranScroll servers.

Some optional features require an internet connection. In particular, optional Google Play purchases require limited purchase and transaction information to be securely transmitted and retained for verification, fraud prevention, aggregate funding progress, and refund handling. These practices are described below.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

To provide core app functionality, QuranScroll stores data locally on your device. This may include:

  • Your reading progress, last-read verse, Juz progress, bookmarks, bookmark categories, daily reading goals, streaks, and reading statistics
  • Your app preferences, including language, translation, Tafsir source, font, reader mode, audio, reminder, and theme settings
  • Your Focus App Blocker settings, including the apps you select and your unlock preferences
  • Downloaded recitation audio, Quran fonts, Tafsir cache, and other cached content used for performance or offline access
  • Custom background images you select from your device
  • Supporter Cards, including a card reference derived from the purchase token, the purchased product, purchase date, and whether the purchase was a test purchase

This locally stored reading and preference data is not uploaded to QuranScroll servers. You can remove local app data through applicable in-app controls, your device's app-storage settings, or by uninstalling QuranScroll. Uninstalling or clearing app storage may permanently remove locally stored data.

3. Optional Google Play Purchases and Funding Goal

QuranScroll offers optional one-time Supporter Card purchases through Google Play Billing. Google Play processes the payment. QuranScroll does not receive or store your payment-card number, bank-account details, or Google account password.

When you initiate or complete a Supporter Card purchase, the app transmits the app package name, purchased product identifier, and Google Play purchase token over an encrypted HTTPS connection to QuranScroll's funding service. The service uses the Google Play Developer API to verify and consume the purchase.

After verification, QuranScroll retains a transaction record containing:

  • A one-way SHA-256 hash of the purchase token; the raw purchase token is not stored in the transaction database
  • The product identifier, Google Play order identifier, purchase time, quantity, and purchase region
  • The transaction status, including whether it was credited, a test purchase, refunded, canceled, or otherwise voided
  • The nominal amount credited to the applicable yearly funding goal

This information is used only to verify purchases, prevent duplicate or fraudulent credits, deliver the Supporter Card, maintain an aggregate yearly funding total, and correctly process cancellations, refunds, and voided purchases. The publicly displayed funding goal contains aggregate totals only and does not expose individual purchase records or purchaser identities.

Purchase verification and transaction records are hosted using Google Cloud services, including Cloud Run and Firestore, and may be processed through Google Play and Google Cloud infrastructure. Google handles information under its own applicable terms and privacy policies.

4. Accessibility Permission and Focus App Blocker

On Android, QuranScroll offers an optional feature called Focus App Blocker. This feature uses Android Accessibility access to detect when an app you selected is opened so QuranScroll can present a Quran reading challenge before allowing access to that app.

If you enable this feature:

  • QuranScroll accesses the package name and app label of apps you choose to block
  • This information is used only to determine when to show the unlock reading flow
  • Accessibility access can be disabled at any time in Android Accessibility settings
  • QuranScroll does not request Android's “display over other apps” permission for this feature
  • QuranScroll does not use Accessibility access to read personal messages, passwords, typed text, or the contents of other apps for advertising, analytics, or profiling

Focus App Blocker selections and activity are handled locally on your device and are not uploaded to QuranScroll servers.

5. Photo Library Access

QuranScroll allows you to select an image for use as a custom background theme through Android's system photo picker.

  • The picker lets you choose a specific image without giving QuranScroll broad access to your photo library
  • QuranScroll does not request camera, legacy storage, or “display over other apps” permission for custom backgrounds
  • The selected image is copied to QuranScroll's private app storage and processed locally on your device
  • The image is not uploaded to QuranScroll servers
  • You can replace or remove the custom background within the app

6. Notifications

QuranScroll may request permission to send notifications for optional reading reminders and Android background-audio controls. Reading reminders can use your locally stored daily goal, progress, streak, preferred reminder time, and saved ayah to create a relevant on-device reminder and return you to your reading position when tapped. Reminder schedules are updated or removed locally when your goal or settings change. This information is not sent to QuranScroll servers. Notification permission is not required to use the main Quran reading features.

7. Audio, Fonts, Tafsir, and Other Online Content

QuranScroll may connect to external content providers to stream or download Quran recitations, word audio, Quran fonts, or Tafsir. Current providers include EveryAyah, Quran.com/Quran Foundation services, and other sources identified in the app's Credits section.

When your device requests content from an external provider, that provider may receive standard network information such as your IP address, request time, requested resource, and basic connection or device information. This is necessary to deliver the requested content and is handled according to the provider's own privacy practices. Downloaded content and caches are stored locally on your device.

The app may also request public Discord community information to display the community invitation and approximate online-member count. If you open Discord, a feedback form, or another external link, the destination service's own privacy policy applies.

8. Technical and Request Information

QuranScroll does not currently use advertising or behavioral analytics SDKs. When the app communicates with QuranScroll's funding service or an external content provider, the service provider may automatically process limited request information, such as IP address, request time, response status, user agent, or similar security and operational logs. This information may be used for service delivery, abuse prevention, debugging, security, and reliability, and is not used by QuranScroll for advertising or behavioral profiling.

9. Third-Party Services

Depending on the features you choose to use, QuranScroll may interact with:

  • Google Play Billing and the Google Play Developer API for optional purchases, purchase verification, and refunds
  • Google Cloud Run, Firestore, and Pub/Sub for secure purchase processing and aggregate funding-goal updates
  • EveryAyah for Quran recitation audio
  • Quran.com/Quran Foundation services for Quran fonts, word audio, and Tafsir content
  • Discord for optional community information and invitations
  • Your operating system and Expo/React Native components for local notifications, audio, the system photo picker, and sharing

These providers may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies. QuranScroll does not authorize these providers to use QuranScroll purchase-verification data for advertising.

10. Data Sharing and Sale

QuranScroll does not sell personal information and does not share your locally stored Quran reading data, bookmarks, Focus App Blocker selections, or custom background images for advertising or marketing. Limited purchase and technical information is processed by Google Play and Google Cloud only as needed to operate the optional purchase feature, maintain service security, and comply with applicable requirements.

11. Data Retention and Requests

Local information remains on your device until you remove it, clear app storage, or uninstall the app. Server-side purchase records may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to prevent duplicate credits, preserve transaction and funding-goal integrity, handle refunds or disputes, maintain security, and meet legal, tax, accounting, or platform obligations.

To ask a question or make a privacy or deletion request concerning server-side purchase information, email fawwynawwy.dev@gmail.com. Include the Google Play order identifier if your request concerns a purchase. We may need to retain limited information where required for transaction integrity, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legal compliance.

12. Data Security

QuranScroll is designed to keep Quran reading activity and most preferences on your device. Purchase-verification requests use HTTPS, raw purchase tokens are replaced by one-way hashes in persistent transaction records, and access to backend records is restricted through Google Cloud access controls. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

13. Children's Privacy

QuranScroll does not require children to create an account or provide a name, email address, or phone number. Optional purchases are processed through Google Play and may be subject to Google Play family purchase approvals and parental controls. Parents and guardians should supervise purchases and device-permission choices made by children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to QuranScroll, contact us using the address below.
