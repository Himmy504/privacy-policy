Privacy Policy for QuranScroll

Last Updated: May 13, 2026

QuranScroll is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app accesses, how it is used, and how that information is handled when you use QuranScroll.

1. Overview

QuranScroll does not require account creation and does not collect or sell personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, or phone number through the app itself.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

To provide core app functionality, QuranScroll stores certain data locally on your device. This may include:

  • Your reading progress, last-read verse, Juz progress, and bookmarks
  • Your app preferences, such as translation settings, font choices, reader mode, and theme selection
  • Your Focus App Blocker settings, including selected blocked apps and unlock preferences
  • Downloaded audio and other cached content used for app performance and offline access

This information is stored locally on your device and is not uploaded to our servers by QuranScroll.

3. Accessibility Permission and Focus App Blocker

On Android, QuranScroll offers an optional feature called Focus App Blocker. This feature uses Android's Accessibility access to detect when selected apps are opened so QuranScroll can present a Quran reading challenge before allowing access to those selected apps.

If you enable this feature:

  • QuranScroll may detect the package name and app label of apps you choose to block
  • QuranScroll uses this information only to determine when to show the unlock reading flow
  • Accessibility access is used only for this optional blocker feature and can be disabled at any time in your Android Accessibility settings
  • QuranScroll does not use Accessibility access to read personal messages, passwords, typed text, or content from other apps for advertising or profiling purposes

Information related to the Focus App Blocker is handled locally on the device and is not sold or shared for marketing purposes.

4. Camera and Photo Library Access

QuranScroll may request access to your device's camera or photo library to allow you to choose or capture an image for use as a custom background theme.

  • Images are selected and processed locally on your device
  • Images are not uploaded to QuranScroll servers by this feature
  • You can remove a custom background at any time within the app

5. Notifications

If you enable reminders, QuranScroll may request permission to send local notifications, such as reading reminders. These notifications are scheduled on your device. QuranScroll does not require notification permission to use the main reading features.

6. Audio Features

QuranScroll includes Quran audio playback and may download or cache recitation audio files on your device to improve playback performance or offline availability. These audio files are stored locally on your device.

7. Automatically Collected Information

The app may process limited technical information necessary for functionality, debugging, compatibility, and performance, such as device type, operating system version, and crash-related information. We do not use this information to personally identify you.

8. Third-Party Services

QuranScroll does not use third-party advertising SDKs and does not sell your personal data. Some app features may rely on platform or library components provided by Android, Expo, or similar infrastructure used to operate app functionality such as notifications, media handling, or file access.

9. Data Sharing

QuranScroll does not sell your personal information. We do not share your locally stored reading data, blocked app selections, or custom background images with third parties for advertising purposes.

10. Data Security

QuranScroll is designed to keep most user data on the device. However, no method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and you use the app at your own discretion.

11. Children's Privacy

QuranScroll is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the “Last Updated” date on this page.
